# Crit 5

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was when I stopped adding more mechanics and focused on making the game clear. Once I gave the water and electricity forms a simple purpose and turned them into short levels, the game started to feel like an actual game instead of just a collection of features.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

This made me think more about how players understand a mechanic without being told what to do. I want to get better at designing interactions where the environment itself gives enough clues, so the player can figure things out by trying them.
